The Craft

Every knot, tied by hand.

Each piece begins as raw cotton cord and takes its shape knot by knot — no molds, no shortcuts, no two pieces quite the same. Mirrors, chairs, lighting and wall art are made to order, so the piece that reaches you is the one made for your space.

Based between Kenya and Tanzania, working with clients across East Africa and beyond.
